The honest truth: For everyday physical store swipes (grocery, retail, clothing), the best you can get from your wallet is 1% (Rs 10 per Rs 1,000). The exciting rates (3.75%, 5%, 7.5%, 10%) all apply to specific online merchants or dining. Your daily kirana/store billing counter tap is always going to be a 1% game — the difference is which 1% card has the fewest exclusions and doesn’t lock you into an ecosystem.
AU Zenith+ wins daily driver because: (1) 1 RP = Rs 1.00 is the best RP value in your wallet — simple math, no devaluation risk. (2) No MCC-based exclusions on grocery/retail/clothing. Critical Cap & Sharing Warnings

BoB Eterna 5X cap is SHARED. The 5,000 bonus RP/cycle limit covers ALL accelerated categories combined — Ecom (Flipkart, Myntra, Amazon) + Dining (MCC 5812) + Travel (MCC 4722). If you spend ₹41.7K on Flipkart, your MakeMyTrip/Cleartrip bookings that cycle earn only 0.75% base. Prioritise the merchant where the 3.75% matters most.
Swiggy BLCK ₹1,500/mo cap is SHARED within categories. Swiggy Food + Swiggy Instamart share one ₹1,500/mo cashback pool (= ₹15,000 spend at 10%). Partner merchants (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra) share a separate ₹1,500/mo pool (= ₹30,000 spend at 5%). Using BLCK heavily on Swiggy Food eats into your Instamart cap and vice versa.
Axis Flipkart ₹4,000/qtr cap. The quarterly cashback cap may be shared across Flipkart + Myntra combined, not separate. Verify from your latest card T&C — if shared, ₹4,000/qtr covers both merchants together.
MCC codes are unreliable. The same merchant can code differently between transactions. Zomato may be 5812 (dining) or 5411 (grocery). Swiggy Instamart uniquely uses 5812 (dining MCC) even for grocery orders sometimes. BoB Eterna earns 3.75% on 5812 but ZERO on 5411 (Annexure A excluded). Always verify via your statement.
