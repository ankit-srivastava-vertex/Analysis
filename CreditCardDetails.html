Critical Cap & Sharing Warnings

BoB Eterna 5X cap is SHARED. The 5,000 bonus RP/cycle limit covers ALL accelerated categories combined — Ecom (Flipkart, Myntra, Amazon) + Dining (MCC 5812) + Travel (MCC 4722). If you spend ₹41.7K on Flipkart, your MakeMyTrip/Cleartrip bookings that cycle earn only 0.75% base. Prioritise the merchant where the 3.75% matters most.
Swiggy BLCK ₹1,500/mo cap is SHARED within categories. Swiggy Food + Swiggy Instamart share one ₹1,500/mo cashback pool (= ₹15,000 spend at 10%). Partner merchants (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra) share a separate ₹1,500/mo pool (= ₹30,000 spend at 5%). Using BLCK heavily on Swiggy Food eats into your Instamart cap and vice versa.
Axis Flipkart ₹4,000/qtr cap. The quarterly cashback cap may be shared across Flipkart + Myntra combined, not separate. Verify from your latest card T&C — if shared, ₹4,000/qtr covers both merchants together.

Fuel Surcharge & Credit Card on UPI

Does paying for fuel via UPI (credit card linked to UPI) attract fuel surcharge? Short answer: No. Here’s why, and what it means for your wallet.

How Fuel Surcharge Works

The mechanism: Fuel surcharge (typically 1% + 18% GST = 1.18%) is triggered when a POS terminal at a fuel station sends MCC 5541/5542 to the card network (Visa/Mastercard/RuPay). UPI transactions bypass the POS terminal entirely — they travel through NPCI UPI rails, not card network POS infrastructure. No POS = no fuel MCC trigger = no surcharge.
